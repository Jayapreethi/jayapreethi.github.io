
# Jaya Preethi Mohan

I believe the best work happens at the edges, where disciplines collide,
where infrastructure meets intelligence, and where the answers are not yet
written down. That is exactly where I choose to work.

I am a researcher, an engineer, and someone who finds genuine joy in
problems that do not have clean solutions yet. My work spans AI and machine
learning, high performance computing, power systems, and cloud infrastructure,
domains where the stakes are real and the questions are hard. My goal is to
keep systems and innovations resilient, secure, reproducible, and accessible.
I have had the privilege of doing this work at a national laboratory, in
doctoral research, and across teams that are shaping how intelligent systems
get built and deployed at scale.
